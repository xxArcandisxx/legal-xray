RENTAL AGREEMENT

This agreement is made between Mrs. S. Reddy (Landlord) and the Tenant for the flat at 5, MG Road, Hyderabad.

1. Monthly rent is Rs 18,000, payable in advance by the 5th of each month.
2. The tenant shall pay a security deposit of Rs 1,80,000. The landlord may deduct any amount from the deposit for any reason and need not provide receipts.
3. The agreement has a lock-in period of 18 months. If the tenant leaves earlier, the tenant must pay rent for the remaining months.
4. The landlord may increase the rent by 15% every 6 months.
5. The tenant shall not have guests or relatives stay overnight without written permission.
6. The landlord may terminate this agreement at any time without giving any reason.
7. The tenant shall pay Rs 2,000 as painting charges when vacating, even if no damage is caused.
