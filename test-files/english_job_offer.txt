OFFER OF EMPLOYMENT

Dear Candidate, we are pleased to offer you the position of Junior Software Engineer at BrightWave Technologies Pvt Ltd.

1. Your annual CTC is Rs 4,20,000, of which 30% is variable pay payable at the sole discretion of the company.
2. You will serve a probation period of 12 months, during which no salary is payable for the first 2 months.
3. You must serve a minimum bond period of 3 years. If you resign earlier, you shall pay Rs 2,00,000 as liquidated damages.
4. The notice period for resignation is 90 days, while the company may terminate your employment with 7 days' notice.
5. For 2 years after leaving, you shall not work for any other company in the software industry.
6. You may be required to work up to 12 hours a day and on weekends without additional pay.
7. All inventions you create, including on personal time, will belong to the company.
